# Research: how hunting and outdoor map apps look and work

Checked 2026-11-27 from public pages only: marketing sites, App Store listings and their screenshots. No sign-ins. Colours and fonts marked "measured" were read from the live page's computed styles; the rest is described from screenshots. Prices are the ones shown that day (several sites were running Black Friday sales, so list prices are given).

The owner asked for "more onX". This file separates what is a convention of the whole genre (safe and wise to follow, so a hunter feels at home) from what is onX's own branding (must not be copied).

## The short version

1. Every app in the genre is a full-screen map with a few floating controls and a sheet that slides up from the bottom. The map is never inside a card.
2. Nearly all of them use dark charcoal plus an orange or red-orange accent: onX, HuntWise, GOHUNT, Garmin Explore and Tread all do. Charcoal and orange is the genre's uniform, not onX's property. onX's own orange is redder (#FF3300, measured) than hunter orange (#FF7A1F).
3. Faint topo contour lines on dark grey are the genre's default decoration (onX, GOHUNT, Gaia, BaseMap). It is so common it identifies nobody. The last brief already dropped it; keep it dropped.
4. Chrome is small and dense. Buttons are 40 to 48 px, labels are short, and numbers (wind, distance, acres, time) are shown as compact readouts. Nothing is padded like a SaaS dashboard.
5. Sheets are layered: a short "peek" state with the two or three main actions, and a taller state with tabs or a list. Layers, tools and waypoint details all open as sheets over the map, so the map stays visible.
6. Basemap choice is always the same three: aerial (satellite), topo, hybrid. It lives in a Layers sheet with picture thumbnails, plus on/off rows for overlays.
7. Marketing sites sell with the map itself: a large map screenshot per feature, one plain sentence each, star rating with the count of ratings, and a plan comparison table with ticks.
8. What reads as trustworthy to hunters: real aerial imagery, exact numbers, plain confident headings, and proof (ratings counts, named publications). What reads as cheap: stock gradients, cartoon illustration, vague copy.

## App by app

### onX Hunt (studied in most depth)

Sources: [onxmaps.com/hunt/app](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app), [features](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features), [pricing](https://www.onxmaps.com/pricing), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/onx-hunt-gps-hunting-maps/id672902340).

- Colour: site chrome is near-black (#222 nav, #111 pricing table) with white text and one red-orange, #FF3300 (measured on the "Start Free Trial" and "Log In" buttons). Light sections are plain white. In the app, the map fills the screen; sheets over it are white in the light look and dark grey in the dark look. Store screenshots sit on charcoal with faint contour lines and a thin orange rule that runs through the logo tile.
- Type: Atlas Grotesk (measured), weight 900 for headings in mixed case on the site. Store screenshots use tall condensed capitals for poster headlines ("PRIVATE AND PUBLIC LANDS"). Body is 16 px.
- Navigation: a top bar on the map with a menu button, the wordmark and search. Round floating buttons at the right edge (weather, basemap "Topo / 2D", locate). A floating pill-shaped tab bar at the bottom: Offline Maps, My Content, Tools, Insights, Go and Track. A "Layers" chip sits just above it.
- Sheets: "Map Layers" is a sheet of grouped rows with a count of active layers ("2 of 15 active"). "Tools" is a sheet with a grid of eight icons with words (Line Distance, Add Waypoint, Mark My Location, Area Shape, Build Route, Compass Mode, Wind Calendar, Add Photo). Tapping a parcel opens a sheet with the owner's name, tabs (Overview, Hunt Unit, Weather, Content) and action buttons along the bottom.
- Waypoints: teardrop pins in strong flat colours (red, blue, yellow, black) with a white glyph. Stand and blind types have their own glyphs.
- People: shared locations are round photo avatars with a green ring and the name in a small white tag above. A banner says who your location is shared with. Live sharing needs cell service ("Requires cellular service" on the features page); offline sharing is limited to sending waypoints.
- Property lines: thin coloured parcel lines over the aerial with owner names in small capitals; public land is tinted by agency.
- Wind: a compass dial drawn around a waypoint, with a wedge for the wind and a row of days marked Good, OK or Bad. A small weather chip on the map shows temperature and wind.
- Sun times: not shown on the main map in any public screenshot.
- Marketing: a photo hero with a heavy headline and one orange button; a sale strip pinned to the bottom of the window; devices cropped in circles; features as "Hundreds of Layers", "Basemaps", "Offline Maps" ("No service? No problem."), "Map Tools". Proof: 4.9 stars from about 275,000 ratings, quotes from named hunters and from Bowhunter, Field and Stream and Outdoor Life.
- Pricing: four columns in one dark table with a "Best Value" flag and rows of ticks. Premium one state $34.99/year, two states $49.99/year, Elite $99.99/year or $14.99/month, 7-day free trial.

### HuntStand

Sources: [huntstand.com](https://www.huntstand.com/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/huntstand-gps-maps-tools/id778772892).

- Dark app chrome (near-black sheets and top bar) with white text; a green accent for selected items; orange teardrop pins with a white disc and a black glyph for stands, blue for water.
- Top bar on the map: back arrow, the hunt area's name with a drop-down, a menu button. Below it one dark "Filter" button.
- The hunt area is a thin orange outline. Food plots and bedding are filled shapes. Parcel taps open a dark sheet with the owner's name.
- It is the app closest to our club features: shared hunt areas and "Stand Reservations" (a Pro feature).
- Marketing: soft, foggy nature photos with a white line icon and one sentence per screenshot; big usage numbers ("2 million active hunters", "210 million acres managed"); press quotes. Pro $29.99/year.

### Spartan Forge

Sources: [spartanforge.ai](https://spartanforge.ai/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spartan-forge-hunt/id1562873100).

- A military look: black panels, a gold-tan and rust palette, capitals with a trailing underscore on buttons ("DOWNLOAD_"), names like "Battlemap" and "Intel".
- The map has a logo bar at the top, a red compass button, a temperature and wind chip, a large compass control at the lower left, and a floating pill tab bar (BFT, Map Layers, Intel, My Content, Tools).
- It is the only one that shows sun times prominently: an "Intel" screen with sunrise, sunset, first light and last light, moon phase and a week of movement bars. It labels first and last light; we must not (sunrise and sunset only).
- From $79.99/year or $12.99/month, 7-day trial.

### BaseMap

Sources: [basemap.com](https://www.basemap.com/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/basemap-3d-hunting-maps-gps/id1305237481).

- Black chrome with a bright green accent (#589500, measured). A status strip above the map shows "GPS Signal" bars and elevation: a small instrument readout.
- Round buttons stacked at the right edge (search, compass, layers, favourites, add). Public land is tinted by agency in strong colours.
- The older, plainer look in the set. It shows the cost of heavy colour tints: the land colours fight the markers.

### HuntWise

Sources: [huntwise.com](https://huntwise.com/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/huntwise-a-better-hunting-app/id645518545).

- Orange (#E7680E, measured) on black and on a torn-paper camo texture. Store headlines are condensed orange capitals.
- The map has a vertical strip of square white buttons at the right edge (GPS, layers, property, radar, search, folders) and an orange "+" at the bottom.
- The property line is a bold orange outline. Stands are orange teardrop pins.
- "WindCast" ranks stands by today's wind in a bottom sheet: a list of stands, each with a percentage and a small wind dial. A chip on the map shows wind speed and the hour.
- Site: a photo hero, "Start Free Trial" in an orange pill, tabs for Map, Forecast and Learn.

### GOHUNT

Sources: [gohunt.com](https://www.gohunt.com/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gohunt-research-maps/id1500630352).

- The most modern type in the set: a wide heavy face (Ringside Wide, measured) in mixed case for headlines, dark grey panels and orange #F26D26 (measured).
- The map has two round buttons and a bottom sheet for recording a track, with large light numerals and small unit labels ("1.34 MI").
- Has chat between members: plain grey and green bubbles. Nothing links a message to the map.

### Gaia GPS

Sources: [gaiagps.com](https://www.gaiagps.com/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gaia-gps-mobile-trail-maps/id1201979492).

- Green brand (#3CC537 and #276338, measured), Inter for type. A topo-first map, so its chrome is light.
- A stats bar across the top of the map while recording: Total Time, Current speed, Distance, Elevation, in cells divided by hairlines. This is the clearest example of the "data fields" pattern.
- A layers button at the lower left; waypoints are round badges with a small tail.
- Site hero: one photo, "Maps for Every Adventure", two buttons: free and premium.

### Garmin Explore and Tread

Sources: [Explore listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garmin-explore/id1276675721), [Tread listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tread/id1518331465).

- Explore: black panels, white text, an orange accent, round black buttons at the right edge (layers, full screen, 3D), a bottom sheet with a place name and a row of readouts (distance, elevation, bearing).
- Tread: square white buttons with rounded corners at the corners of the map. Group ride shows each rider as a coloured arrow with the name in plain text beside it, and a banner: "Sharing your location with Group Ride". It works over Garmin's own radios, the nearest thing in the set to what we do, and it shows people very simply.
- Garmin handhelds are where the data-field row comes from: a strip of labelled readouts across the top of the map.

## Genre conventions: follow these

| Convention | Seen in | What we do with it |
|---|---|---|
| Full-screen map; nothing boxes it in | All | The map fills the phone, the site hero, the sign-in page and the admin's main pane |
| Aerial imagery by default | onX, HuntStand, HuntWise, Spartan Forge | Aerial is the default basemap |
| Dark chrome over the map, with a light option | onX, HuntStand, Garmin, GOHUNT | Dark first; a real daylight theme |
| Orange as the action colour | onX, HuntWise, GOHUNT, Garmin | Hunter orange #FF7A1F, kept to three jobs |
| A stack of controls on the right edge | onX, HuntWise, BaseMap, Garmin | Layers, North, Find me, each with a word |
| Bottom sheet with a short and a tall state | All | Check In and Ask for a Hand in the short state; layers in the tall state |
| Layers sheet: three basemap thumbnails, then on/off rows | onX, Gaia, Garmin | Same |
| A row of readouts in hairline-divided cells | Gaia, Garmin, BaseMap, GOHUNT | Our strip: sunrise, sunset, wind, temperature |
| A coloured ring around a person | onX (green ring on avatars) | A ring that means something: solid green heard just now, broken gold not lately |
| A tag beside each marker with the name | onX, Tread | A two-line plate: where, then who and how long ago |
| A wedge for wind at a point | onX, HuntWise | The scent cone from your own dot |
| Bottom tab bar with icon and word | onX, Spartan Forge | Four tabs, docked |
| Site: the map shown large, one sentence per feature | onX, HuntStand, Spartan Forge | The hero is the map; the features are a map legend |
| Site: a comparison table with ticks | onX | One table, Free against Club |
| Free first, paid clearly labelled, a trial | HuntStand, onX, Spartan Forge | Free app first; club price and the 14-day trial stated plainly |

## onX-specific branding: do not copy

- The names "onX", "onX Hunt", "Elite", "Premium" for plans, "Go and Track", "Hunt Research Tools", "Whitetail Insights", "TerrainX", and the feature names of the others ("WindCast", "HuntCast", "RutCast", "Battlemap", "Intel", "HuntZone").
- The mark: a white X in an orange-red square tile, and "ON [X] HUNT" lockups.
- The colour #FF3300 with white text on it. Ours is #FF7A1F with near-black text, which also passes contrast where white would not.
- The thin orange rule that runs across the top of their store art through the logo tile, and the charcoal-with-contours backdrop behind it.
- Atlas Grotesk, and tall condensed capitals for headlines.
- The floating pill tab bar with five tools, and the wordmark bar across the top of the map.
- Teardrop pins in their flat colour set, and round photo avatars for people.
- Devices cropped in circles on the site, and the sale strip pinned to the bottom of the window.
- Parcel owner names and agency colour tints. We have no parcel data, and should not look as if we do.

## What none of them do (our opening)

- None shows a party live with no cell service on an ordinary phone. onX says live sharing needs cellular service. Garmin needs Garmin hardware.
- None says how fresh a position is. A ring that goes from solid green to broken gold, with "2 min ago" beside it, is ours.
- None shows radio or mesh status, because none has one.
- None has check-in, open and occupied stands, or an after-sunset alert tied to the map. HuntStand's stand reservations are the nearest.
- None puts sunrise and sunset on the map itself, although every hunter plans around them.
- Most paywall the map layers. Our line is the opposite: everything over the radios is free.

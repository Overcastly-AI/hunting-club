What it does when the bars drop to zero

  • A live map that works offline

    Free USGS topo and aerial maps, downloaded to your phone before you go. Your party on the map with no cell service.

  • Check in your way

    At a spot, a log or a field edge, or on the move while you still-hunt. Switch without checking out.

  • Ask for a Hand and overdue alerts

    Ask for a Hand sends your location to your party and keeps trying until someone answers. If you are still out after sunset plus your grace time, your party hears about it.

  • Chat over the radio

    Party chat, quick replies and direct messages, with no cell service.

  • Harvest log

    Log a harvest in a few taps. It stays private unless you share it.

How it works

  1. Get a Meshtastic radio

    Any radio with GPS that runs stock Meshtastic firmware. Sold separately.

  2. Pair it with the app

    The app connects over Bluetooth and sets the radio up for you.

  3. Start or join a party

    Scan a Party QR code. A free party has up to 3 people.

Questions

Do I need cell service?

No. The map, chat, check-ins and Ask for a Hand travel over the radios.

Is it an emergency service?

No. It helps your party look out for each other. For emergencies, call 911 from your phone if you have service.

Is it free?

Yes, for you and a party of up to 3. Clubs pay for the shared setup and admin tools.

Does it show when I can legally hunt?

No. It shows sunrise and sunset for where you are. The rules are your state's.
